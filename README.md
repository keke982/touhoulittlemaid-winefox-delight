# 🍷 Winefox Delight

**Raise them. Befriend them. Feed them. Or face the consequences.**

Winefox Delight is an extensive add-on for Touhou Little Maid that transforms ordinary maid management into a deeper, more dynamic, and occasionally disturbing experience.

Build a thriving maid farm, breed new generations, explore social interactions, prepare food, and discover what happens when the peaceful life of your maids takes a darker turn.

From cute daily activities to unsettling consequences, your choices can shape the stories that unfold on your farm.

## 🌱 Features

### 🏡 Maid Farming & Natural Spawning
- Wild maids can naturally spawn throughout the world.
- Maid groups may appear around houses and village environments.
- Befriend wild maids with sweet berries and bring them to your farm.
- Provide food, shelter, and suitable living conditions to maintain your growing population.

### 🍼 Breeding & Generational Growth
- Adult maids can breed and produce baby maids.
- Baby maids gradually grow into adults.
- Some appearance traits can be inherited from their parents.
- Build a farm with multiple generations of maids.

### 🍰 Food, Cooking & Resource Management
- Maids interact with food and may compete with each other for meals.
- Certain foods can attract groups of maids and trigger special interactions.
- Explore additional food-related gameplay through supported cooking and crafting integrations.
- Manage your farm's food supply as your maid population grows.

### 💬 Social Behavior & Emotional Reactions
- Maids have contextual dialogue and reactions.
- Their behavior can change in response to nearby events.
- Maid groups can exhibit protective behavior, conflicts, and other social interactions.
- Certain events may lead to fear, sadness, resentment, or rebellion.

### 🔪 Butchery & Processing
- The mod explores a darker side of maid farming through butchery-related mechanics.
- Certain gameplay systems involve processing maid-related remains and integrating them into the broader food and crafting experience.
- Dedicated interactions and dialogue react to disturbing events around the farm.
- These mechanics add a grim contrast to the otherwise peaceful breeding and farming systems.

### 🕯️ Despair, Mourning & Supernatural Events
- Witnessing the deaths of other maids can affect the emotional state of surviving maids.
- Repeated traumatic events may lead to depression, refusal to work, or rebellion.
- Unattended remains can trigger mourning-related events and other unsettling consequences.
- Certain locations may become associated with supernatural phenomena after dark.
- Mysterious figures and disturbing encounters can turn an ordinary farm into a horror experience.

### ⚔️ Grudges, Revenge & Consequences
- Certain actions can cause maids to develop resentment toward the player.
- Grudges can lead to hostile behavior, sabotage, and revenge-related interactions.
- Some situations allow a conflict to end before it escalates further.
- Treat your maids carelessly, and the consequences may eventually catch up with you.

- ### 😾 Grudge System — Actions Have Consequences

Maids have memories, feelings, and limits. Treat them poorly, and they may not forget it.

Winefox Delight features a persistent grudge system that tracks how a maid feels about the player's behavior. Hitting, bullying, neglecting, or failing to meet a maid's basic needs can gradually increase her grudge value.

As resentment grows, her behavior changes through several stages:

- **Displeased:** She may avoid the player, display angry reactions, and express her dissatisfaction through dialogue.
- **Holding a Grudge:** She may secretly stare at the player, become less willing to flee when attacked, and occasionally sabotage household objects such as doors or chests.
- **Revenge:** Further aggression can trigger a retaliatory fight. The maid may pursue and attack the player before eventually stopping and delivering a final line of dialogue.

Grudges are not necessarily permanent. Feeding the maid, interacting with her, and spending time together can gradually reduce her resentment. However, reconciliation becomes more difficult when her grudge value is high.

The system also accounts for prolonged neglect and certain poor living conditions, making everyday care an important part of maintaining a good relationship.

**Be careful how you treat your maids. Forgiveness is possible, but it is not guaranteed to come easily.**

### ⚔️ Rebel Maids — An Armed Resistance

Not every maid is willing to remain under someone else's control.

Winefox Delight features a separate faction of armed rebel maids, complete with their own camps, equipment, and hostile behavior. These are not simply ordinary maids with a different appearance: they operate as an independent armed force within the game world.

- **Armed Combat:** Rebel maids can carry firearms and attack nearby players or hostile creatures. With compatible weapon mods installed, they can use weapons such as the M4A1 and may throw grenades. Without the required firearm integration, they can use a melee weapon instead.
- **Rebel Camps:** Rebel encampments provide a home for the faction and serve as places where players can encounter its members.
- **Disarmament and Negotiation:** Players can approach a rebel camp while holding a white flag to initiate a truce. The rebels will confiscate weapons, ammunition, grenades, and food carried by the player.
- **Truce Rules:** Disarmament establishes a temporary peaceful arrangement, but the agreement can be broken if the player draws a weapon inside the camp or attacks a maid.
- **Independent Combat Behavior:** Rebel maids can engage nearby threats without requiring the player to command every action.

Rebel maids add a different dimension to the mod's world, introducing armed encounters, faction-based interactions, and the possibility of negotiating with a group that does not automatically trust the player.

**Enter their territory carefully. A white flag may open the door to negotiation, but breaking the truce has consequences.**

### ⚙️ Configuration & Compatibility
Various settings allow players to customize aspects of spawning, breeding, and maid ecology.

The mod also integrates with several companion mods to expand animations, activities, crafting, and food-related gameplay.

## 📦 Requirements & Dependencies

**Minecraft:** 1.20.1  
**Mod Loader:** Minecraft Forge

Required dependencies:

1. **Touhou Little Maid** — Core maid system.
2. **MoreAnimation** — Additional animations.
3. **Maid Cute Activity** — Maid activities and interactions.
4. **ButcherCraft** — Butchery-related gameplay integration.
5. **Farmer's Delight** — Food and cooking integration.

Install compatible versions of all required dependencies before launching the game.

# 📢 Project Update — Moving to GitHub

**Winefox Delight is now available on GitHub!**

After experiencing repeated reports, removals, and review issues on CurseForge, I've decided to make GitHub an additional home for this project.

## Why GitHub?

Winefox Delight is an unconventional Touhou Little Maid add-on. It combines maid farming, breeding, daily interactions, cooking integration, and darker gameplay mechanics involving butchery, emotional reactions, revenge, and horror-inspired events.

Unfortunately, the project has repeatedly faced reports and removals on CurseForge. Maintaining the project under these circumstances has become increasingly frustrating, especially when updates and development are interrupted.

I understand that not everyone will enjoy the themes and mechanics featured in this mod. That's completely fine — this project is not intended for everyone.

However, I would prefer players to evaluate the mod based on its actual content rather than having its availability repeatedly disrupted by disagreements over its design.

## Why GitHub?

Moving to GitHub gives the project another place to host releases, maintain version history, publish changelogs, and organize bug reports.

It also makes it easier to keep older versions available and communicate directly with players about development.

Please note that GitHub has its own policies and rules. This move is intended to provide a more convenient and reliable development and distribution channel, not to bypass platform policies.

## What's Next?
